Watch the video to practice blending the short /o/ sound with \*\*log\*\*, \*\*dog\*\*, and \*\*hop\*\* out loud, then print the worksheet so your child can trace, match, and blend short /o/ words on paper.

\### Screen-Free Worksheet & Play Steps

1. \*\*Familiar Warm-Up:\*\* Start with \*\*log\*\*, \*\*dog\*\*, and \*\*hop\*\* from the video so your child spots words they already know.

2. \*\*Hands-On Blending:\*\* Have your child trace the letters and sound out extra short /o/ words like \*\*pot\*\*, \*\*hot\*\*, \*\*mop\*\*, and \*\*fox\*\* on their own.

3. \*\*Tactile Game:\*\* Have your child circle the picture that matches each word or match the beginning letter sound to the right short /o/ ending.
